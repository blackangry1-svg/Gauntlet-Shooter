# First-floor corridor trial
Double-click PlayEarthTrial.cmd in this folder.
It starts with a separate trial save and leaves your existing adventure progress intact.
PlayAdventure.cmd continues your regular hero; old acquired weapons remain usable.
Earth passages are 9 metres wide, three times the original width, with 5.4-metre walls and a sky.
The original maze, keys, gates, treasure, shortcuts and boss exit remain.
Water, Fire, Air and Aether retain their existing geometry and encounter populations.
## Controls
WASD moves; mouse looks; left click attacks; right click uses a heavy attack.
E interacts; Space dodges; Q guards with the buckler; F uses a flask; Shift sprints.
1 through 5 select weapons; brackets cycle eligible weapons.
Tab opens the map and journal; Escape opens settings; F5 saves; F9 shows performance.
Controller: left stick move/right stick look; A interact; B dodge; X flask; Y heavy; shoulders attack/guard; D-pad cycle; Start menu; Back journal.
## Width and combat test
Walk the shoreline and collect the starting chest. Compare room to dodge and sidestep.
Enter the maze and try sharing a hallway with a mixed squad of three.
Find the key, open the full-width gate, and use the far-side lever to return.
Under-level enemies pursue through reachable corridors. A much stronger hero makes weaker foes cautious.
Return to a marked refuge; enemy pursuit and hostile shots stop in its sanctuary.
Press E at the refuge to rest and open the stat choices.
XP levels the hero; Embers still buy weapon and utility upgrades from the smith.
Each level raises Strength, Dexterity or Focus by one. Weapon requirements appear in the journal.
Sword requires Strength6; bow Dexterity6; staff Focus6; spear Strength7 and Dexterity6.
The driftwood always remains available. Renewable regular enemies provide more XP when needed.
Save with F5, quit, relaunch the trial and confirm the same equipment, level and opened passages.
Test the Earth guardian after trying group combat; note any doorway or passage that feels restrictive.
## CPU settings and feedback
Escape -> Quality cycles balanced, native, low power. Low power renders60%, caps45FPS and activates at most8 regular enemies; balanced10 and native12. A boss is additional.
F9 shows actual FPS and active counts. Measured development timings are samples, not an FPS guarantee.
Please report passage comfort, squad movement, pursuit/refuge behavior and any slowdowns.
Larger boss chambers, lakes and parkour rooms are the next design pass after this width trial.
